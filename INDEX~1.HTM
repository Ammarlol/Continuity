Upload a Moodle question-bank export (a whole quiz, or a single question). Continuity automatically finds any Formulas-type question where one part's correct answer algebraically depends on an earlier part, and adds Error-Carry-Forward grading — so a student who makes one early slip but reasons correctly afterwards isn't marked wrong on every part that follows. Everything runs in your browser; your file is never uploaded anywhere.
